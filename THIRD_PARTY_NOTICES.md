# Third-party data notices

## Predictive bigram tables

My Switcher bundles the English, Russian and Ukrainian top-50,000 ordered bigram tables from [synth-inc/predictive-bigrams](https://github.com/synth-inc/predictive-bigrams), pinned to commit `76001baff94965b9cde7e07a4b0f007ead013a1d`.

The tables are derived from the Leipzig Corpora Collection / Deutscher Wortschatz, University of Leipzig:
https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/

The upstream project states that its derived bigram tables are redistributed under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license. The files contain lowercased word pairs and corpus frequencies, not complete sentences.

Attribution: Leipzig Corpora Collection, University of Leipzig; derived table compilation by synth-inc/predictive-bigrams.

Upstream data project: https://github.com/synth-inc/predictive-bigrams
